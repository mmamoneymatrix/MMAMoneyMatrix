4.5 Why Bias Matrices Matter
Without judge bias:

Every simulation would produce too clean decisions.

You’d miss:

weird split decisions

grappling vs striking disagreements

control vs damage debates

With bias matrices:

You get:

realistic split decisions

fights where one judge “saw it differently”

more variance in decision outcomes

better modeling of controversial fights

This is what makes MMAMoneyMatrix feel like actual UFC judging, not just math.
