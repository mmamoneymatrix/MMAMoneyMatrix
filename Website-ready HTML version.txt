<section id="how-it-works">
  <h1>How MMAMoneyMatrix Works</h1>

  <p>MMAMoneyMatrix is a modular MMA fight simulation engine built for real-world prediction, not just entertainment.</p>

  <h2>1. Fighter Data</h2>
  <p>
    We start by building a complete profile for each fighter:
    striking stats, grappling stats, fight history, style tags,
    gym tier, and underdog level. All data is normalized and stored
    in a structured database.
  </p>

  <h2>2. Matchup Logic Engine</h2>
  <p>
    The matchup engine analyzes how two fighters interact:
    pressure vs counter, wrestler vs striker, kick-heavy vs grappler,
    cardio monster vs slow starter, and more. It produces a
    <strong>fight geometry</strong> object that describes range, pace,
    grappling advantage, striking advantage, chaos, cardio, and
    vulnerability.
  </p>

  <h2>3. Monte Carlo Simulation</h2>
  <p>
    Using the fight geometry, we simulate the fight thousands of times.
    Each simulation runs round-by-round, modeling damage, control,
    knockdowns, submissions, fatigue, and momentum. This produces raw
    win, method, and round probabilities.
  </p>

  <h2>4. Finish Probability Engine</h2>
  <p>
    We model KO/TKO and submission outcomes using power, knockdown rate,
    submission threat, chaos factor, vulnerability, and fatigue.
    This determines inside-the-distance results and when they occur.
  </p>

  <h2>5. Scoring & Judging Model</h2>
  <p>
    If a simulated fight goes the distance, we apply a realistic UFC-style
    scoring model: effective striking, effective grappling, aggression,
    and octagon control. We simulate multiple judges with different bias
    profiles to produce decision outcomes like unanimous, split, and
    majority decisions.
  </p>

  <h2>6. Bonus Layer</h2>
  <p>
    We add realism with two bonus systems:
    an <strong>Elite Gym Tier Bonus</strong> and an
    <strong>Underdog Bonus</strong>. These reflect real-world trends
    where elite camps and live underdogs outperform raw stats.
  </p>

  <h2>7. Final Output</h2>
  <p>
    Finally, we normalize all probabilities and generate:
    win probability, method probability, round probability,
    confidence intervals, and an AI-powered fight breakdown that
    explains why the matchup looks the way it does.
  </p>
</section>
