2. Matchup Logic Engine (Advanced Breakdown)
The Matchup Logic Engine is the brain of MMAMoneyMatrix.
It takes two fighters — each with hundreds of attributes — and determines how they interact before the Monte Carlo simulation begins.

Think of it as the pre‑fight physics model that sets the stage for everything that follows.

Below is the full, advanced breakdown.

🟥 2.1 Purpose of the Matchup Logic Engine
The engine answers one question:

“How do these two fighters interact stylistically, physically, and historically?”
It does this by:

Comparing stats

Comparing styles

Comparing tendencies

Comparing historical patterns

Applying matchup modifiers

Generating a “fight geometry” profile

This profile is then fed into the Monte Carlo simulation.

🟥 2.3 Core Matchup Components
The engine evaluates six major matchup categories, each with multiple sub‑layers.

🟦 2.3.1 Style vs Style Interaction (Deep Layer)
This is the most important part of the engine.

Your style system creates behavioral vectors for each fighter.
The matchup engine compares these vectors to determine:

A. Range Control
Kick-heavy vs boxer

Long reach vs short reach

Southpaw vs orthodox

Pressure vs counter

B. Pace Interaction
Cardio monster vs slow starter

Volume striker vs low-volume power striker

Pressure striker vs point fighter

C. Grappling Interaction
Wrestler vs striker

Grappler vs scrambler

Top-control fighter vs bottom player

Clinch-heavy vs distance striker

D. Chaos Interaction
Brawler vs technician

Fast starter vs slow starter

KO artist vs durable fighter

Each interaction produces positive or negative modifiers.

Example:

Code
pressure_striker vs counter_striker → +0.15 countering advantage
kick_heavy vs wrestler → -0.10 takedown vulnerability
cardio_monster vs slow_starter → +0.20 late-round advantage
